# 💼 LinkedIn Post Guide — step by step (no technical background needed)

This takes about 10 minutes. Post the **article first** (so you have a link for the
comment), or post now and add the link comment later — either is fine.

> **One important rule:** do **not** mention any hackathon/competition in the post or the
> hashtags. This is a project showcase, not a competition post.

---

## Step 1 — Copy the post text

Open this file in the project folder:

`content/linkedin.md`

You will see a block of text between two `---` lines. **Copy only the text between them**
(it starts with “Most support "AI" is a goldfish…” and ends with the hashtags line).
Do **not** copy the title lines above it.

**In a hurry?** Here is the exact text to copy — just replace nothing:

```
Most support "AI" is a goldfish with a confidence problem.

My agent flagged a regression we'd already marked fixed. Here's what changed:

• Wrote each ticket to two banks: one per customer, one shared for the company
• Retained after replying, recalled before the next — async extraction will bite you
• Used tags, not metadata, to isolate customer data
• Let Hindsight consolidate recurring tickets into observations with a proof count
• Read a live "Emerging Issues" report with no LLM call

Before: "Please clear your cache."
After: "This is a known export issue on v2.4; here's the workaround."

Choosing Hindsight agent memory was the difference between remembering and learning.

#AIAgents #AgentMemory #AIMemory #LLM #Hindsight
```

---

## Step 2 — Post it on LinkedIn

1. Open your browser and go to **https://www.linkedin.com** and sign in.
2. Click **“Start a post”** (near the top of your feed).
3. Click inside the text box and **paste** (Ctrl+V on Windows, Cmd+V on Mac).
4. Skim it once to make sure it looks right.
5. Click **Post**.

---

## Step 3 — Add the important links as the FIRST comment

(The post text itself must not lead with links, so we add them in a comment.)

1. Under your new post, click **Comment**.
2. Paste this and edit the `<...>` part if your article is already online:

```
Here's the full write-up if you want the design details: <PASTE YOUR ARTICLE LINK HERE>

Hindsight is open source — repo here: https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight
```

3. Click the **send arrow** to publish the comment.

> If your article isn’t published yet, write the comment with just the Hindsight link for
> now, and edit it later to add the article link (click the three dots `…` on your
> comment → **Edit**).

---

## Step 4 — Confirm it looks good

- Your post shows the text and 5 hashtags.
- Your first comment shows the Hindsight link (and the article link once ready).
- Nothing says “hackathon”.

That’s it. **Screenshot the post and send it to the team.**
