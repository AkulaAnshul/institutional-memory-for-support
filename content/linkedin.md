Most support "AI" is a goldfish with a confidence problem.

My agent flagged a regression we'd already marked fixed. Here's what changed:

• Wrote each ticket to two banks: one per customer, one shared for the company
• Retained after replying, recalled before the next — async extraction will bite you
• Used tags, not metadata, to isolate customer data
• Let Hindsight consolidate recurring tickets into observations with a proof count
• Read a live "Emerging Issues" report with no LLM call

Before: "Please clear your cache."
After: "This is a known export issue on v2.4; here's the workaround."

Choosing Hindsight agent memory was the difference between remembering and learning.
